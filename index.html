The Hacienda opens its doors

Fifteen vendors, a rooftop bar over Clematis, and a 130-seat patio with a stage, next door to Respectable Street. Named for the Factory Records club in Manchester. Built around an olive tree from the early 1980s.

Since the 1950s · West Palm Beach

A counter, a griddle and a neon sign that has outlasted nearly everything around it. Breakfast runs all day, the cakes are cut by the slice, and the cooks work where you can watch them.
